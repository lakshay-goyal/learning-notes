# Industry and engineering research

Use when deployment experience, operational limits, incidents, scale, or design rationale materially improves the lesson.

Prefer reports from the team that built or operated the system, incident postmortems, maintainer talks/discussions, design documents, and reproducible performance investigations. Use community discussions mainly to discover questions or illustrate bounded experiences.

For each analyzed case, extract:

- problem and constraints
- why the technology/design was selected
- architecture and integration decisions
- failures, limitations, or operational surprises
- performance/scale evidence and measurement context
- alternatives considered
- what generalizes and what is organization-specific

Label statements in the lesson:

- **Documented practice:** directly supported by the cited organization/maintainer.
- **Engineering inference:** a reasoned conclusion not stated by the source.
- **Educational example:** a constructed scenario, never described as a real deployment.

Completion condition: included cases answer a planned question and their evidence boundary is clear. Omit industry material when no reliable case adds value.
