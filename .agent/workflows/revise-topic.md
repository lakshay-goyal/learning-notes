# Revise a topic

Open the topic's active-recall section before rereading. Attempt due questions, reveal answers only after retrieval, and record the browser-local self-assessment. Use `Forgot`, `Difficult`, `Remembered`, or `Easy` to schedule the next local review.

For a deeper review, use the original `docs/<slug>/revision.md` prompts interactively. Browser progress is a convenience layer; the research package remains unchanged and cross-device sync is not available.
